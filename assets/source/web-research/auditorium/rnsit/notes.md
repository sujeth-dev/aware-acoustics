## Institution
- **Name:** RNS Institute of Technology (RNSIT), part of the R N Shetty Group of Educational Institutions
- **Location:** Dr. Vishnuvardhan Road, Channasandra, Rajarajeshwari Nagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India
- **Type:** Private engineering college (autonomous, affiliated to VTU)

## Auditorium facts
- Public sources describe RNSIT's on-campus auditorium as having roughly **1,800 seats**
  (per aggregator sites), while other listings reference a "Dr. R.N. Shetty Auditorium"
  (~1,200+ capacity) and a smaller "Dr. R.N. Shetty Mini Auditorium." Figures are
  inconsistent across secondary sources; none is an authoritative primary citation.
- This roughly aligns with, but does not exactly match, the 1,400-seat figure already
  recorded in `data/projects.json` for slug `education-auditorium-bengaluru` — the
  discrepancy is noted, not resolved, by this research pass.
