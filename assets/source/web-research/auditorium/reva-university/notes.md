# REVA University — Auditorium (research notes)

**Status:** Research complete (exterior + interior found)

## Institution
- **Name:** REVA University
- **Location:** Kattigenahalli, Yelahanka, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India (43-acre campus, near Bangalore International Airport)
- **Type:** Private deemed-to-be university (multi-disciplinary: engineering, management, law, architecture, etc.)

## Auditorium facts
- REVA's main campus has multiple auditoria. The largest is the **Kuvempu Auditorium**, located in the CV Raman Block, with a seating capacity of approximately **1,500**.
- A second, smaller auditorium — the **Chanakya Auditorium** (RBS Block) — seats 500+.
- The campus also has an open-air amphitheatre ("REVA Rangasthala") in the Swami Vivekananda Block.
- No public source confirms which auditorium (if any) is the one referenced in an Aware Acoustics engagement; this is general public-facts research only, not a confirmed project match.
