# Somaiya Vidyavihar University — Auditorium (research notes)

**Status:** Research complete (exterior + interior found)

## Institution
- **Name:** Somaiya Vidyavihar University (parent body of the K J Somaiya group of colleges,
  including K J Somaiya College of Engineering / K J Somaiya Institute of Technology)
- **Location:** Vidyavihar, Ghatkopar East, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India (60-acre campus)
- **Type:** Private deemed-to-be university; multi-institution educational trust founded 1959

## Identification note
"Somaiah University" as given in the brief has no exact match; the well-known institution
matching that description is **Somaiya Vidyavihar University** (K J Somaiya group), Mumbai.
No public evidence was found of a Bengaluru Somaiya campus, so Mumbai/Vidyavihar is treated
as the correct identification for this research pass.

## Auditorium facts
- The Vidyavihar campus has several named auditoria: **Takshila** and **Nalanda** (250
  seats each, at K J Somaiya Institute of Management), the **Smt. Sakarben Sabhagraha**
  (400 seats), and a well-equipped auditorium at K J Somaiya College of Engineering (KJSCE)
  seating approximately 450–950 depending on source. There is also a Tagore Amphitheatre
  and Gargi Plaza used for cultural events.
- No public source ties a specific named auditorium here to an Aware Acoustics engagement;
  this is general public-facts research only.
