# HN Science Centre — Auditorium (research notes)

**Status:** Research complete (exterior + interior found), with identification caveat

## Institution
- **Name:** Dr. H N Science Centre — full name "Dr. H. Narasimhaiah Science Centre"
  (also seen as "HN Science Park" / "H N Science Center")
- **Location:** Hosur village, Gauribidanur taluk, Chikkaballapur district, Karnataka,
  India (on NH 234). Hosur is the birthplace of the centre's namesake.
- **Type:** Government-backed regional science education/interactive science centre,
  named after physicist, educationist and rationalist **Dr. H. Narasimhaiah**
  (1920–2005), former Vice-Chancellor of Bangalore University and Padma Bhushan
  recipient (1984).

## Identification note
The brief's "likely Nehru Science Centre" guess does not match: Nehru Science Centre is
a large, separate institution in Worli, Mumbai. The correct "HN" match found in Karnataka
is **Dr. H Narasimhaiah Science Centre**, Gauribidanur — "HN" being the namesake's
initials, not "Nehru." This is treated as the correct identification for this research pass.

## Auditorium facts
- The centre is part of a planned larger science-park development (132 acres allocated,
  announced 2018) with a planetarium dome, physics/aeronautics/biology laboratories, a
  20-acre botanical garden, and an on-site auditorium.
- A local news report (Chikkaballapur.com, March 2022) documents the formal inauguration
  of the centre's **auditorium, mini-planetarium and galleries**, attended by state
  officials; no seating-capacity figure was found in public sources.
- The centre sits near other scientific facilities (BARC seismic array, TIFR gravitational
  lab, RRI radio observatory, IIAP) within a ~10 km radius.
