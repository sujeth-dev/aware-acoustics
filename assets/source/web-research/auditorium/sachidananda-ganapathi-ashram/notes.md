# Sachidananda Ganapathi Ashram — Auditorium/Assembly Hall (research notes)

**Status:** Research complete (exterior + interior found)

## Institution
- **Name:** Avadhoota Datta Peetham, home of Sri Ganapathy Sachchidananda Ashrama
  (also written "Sri Ganapati Sachchidananda Ashrama")
- **Location:** Mysuru–Ooty Highway, Mysuru (Mysore), Karnataka, India
- **Type:** Hindu spiritual ashram/monastery, founded 6 June 1966 by Sri Ganapathy
  Sachchidananda Swamiji; today a large ashram/temple complex with multiple shrines,
  gardens (Shuka Vana bird park), and a dedicated music/assembly auditorium.

## Auditorium facts
- The ashram's purpose-built auditorium is the **Nada Mantapa** ("Hall of Musical Tones"),
  also referred to as the "Raga Ragini Samudaya Bhavan." It covers approximately
  **35,000 sq ft** and is equipped with sound, lighting and theatrical systems.
- Inaugurated 31 May 1998 by Sri Ganapathy Sachchidananda Swamiji; has hosted thousands
  of concerts since. The ashram also has a separate "Universal Prayer Hall" used for
  daily congregational prayers and large religious functions.
- No public source confirms an Aware Acoustics engagement here; this is general
  public-facts research only.
