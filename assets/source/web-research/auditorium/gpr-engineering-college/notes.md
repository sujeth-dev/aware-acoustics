## Institution (as identified)
- **Name:** G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College (Autonomous) (GPREC)
- **Location:** Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, India (on Kurnool–Nandyal Road, ~30-acre campus)
- **Type:** Autonomous engineering college, founded by the G. Pulla Reddy Educational
  Trust (G. Pulla Reddy was a Hyderabad/Kurnool businessman, founder of the Pulla Reddy
  Sweets chain).

## Auditorium facts
- Public sources confirm GPREC has an on-campus auditorium (located near the food court)
  plus multiple departmental seminar halls, but no seating-capacity figure is available
  from public sources.
- The domed rotunda building pictured in exterior.jpg is labelled "Smt./Sri G. Pulla
  Reddy Memorial" on campus signage — a likely candidate for the college's main hall/
  auditorium building, but this has not been independently confirmed as the auditorium.
