## Identification

The Aditya Birla Group's residential real-estate arm is **Birla Estates** (a subsidiary of Century
Textiles and Industries Ltd, launched 2016). Its most prominent/most-documented Mumbai tower is
**Birla Niyaara**, and specifically its signature tower **SILAS**. This is the project selected here
as the most findable match for the spreadsheet's generic "Aditya Birla" label — the original deck
does not specify which Birla property Aware worked on, so this cannot be confirmed as the same
project without client-side clarification.

## Location

Century Mills Compound, Pandurang Budhkar Marg, Worli, Mumbai 400030, Maharashtra, India.

## Summary

Birla Niyaara is a single ultra-luxury residential tower developed by Birla Estates on a ~10-acre
former mill-land site in Worli, South Mumbai, offering 2–7 BHK apartments including the signature
SILAS tower's bespoke 4 & 5 BHK residences, marketed as India's first LEED Pre-Certified Platinum
residential project. It sits directly on the sea-facing side of Worli overlooking the Bandra–Worli
Sea Link, with multiple in-house clubhouses (HIVE, PLAYPEN, BE-FIT). Reported completion is 2028;
some phases/marketing materials describe it as ongoing/under construction, so status should not be
treated as "completed" without further confirmation.
