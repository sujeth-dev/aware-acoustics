# Inorbit Mall — Malad, Mumbai

**TYPOLOGY MISMATCH — READ FIRST:** Inorbit Mall is a **retail shopping mall**, not a residential
tower or township. The source spreadsheet/deck lists it under the same "Residential Segment" bullet
group as Kalpataru and Aditya Birla (deck slide: "Residential Segment ... Kalpataru Real Estate /
Aditya Birla / In orbit — Rahejas"), but that is a mis-grouping in the source material. Inorbit Mall
should not be filed, categorised, or published as a residential project. It is included here only
because the task brief required researching it under the "residential" research folder to match the
spreadsheet's (incorrect) grouping.

**Research only — not for publication.**

## Identification

Inorbit Mall, Malad — the flagship and first property of Inorbit Malls (India) Pvt. Ltd., a
subsidiary of **K Raheja Corp** ("Rahejas" in the source deck, confirming the developer link even
though the typology is wrong).

## Location

New Link Road, Malad West, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

## Summary

Inorbit Mall Malad opened in 2004 as K Raheja Corp's first shopping mall and is the fourth-oldest
mall in Mumbai. It has a gross leasable area of about 500,000 sq ft, was designed by architectural
firm P.G. Patki and Associates, and houses 125+ retail brands (Zara, H&M, Uniqlo, Shoppers Stop,
PVR INOX among others) around a large central atrium with a domed skylight and multi-level
escalator/staircase core. Inorbit has since grown into a chain of malls across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai
(Vashi), Hyderabad, Vadodara and other cities, all owned and operated by K Raheja Corp.
