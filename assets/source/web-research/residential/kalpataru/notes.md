## Identification

Kalpataru Limited (Kalpataru Group) is a Mumbai-based developer with 50+ years in residential and
commercial real estate. The spreadsheet/deck entry only says "Kalpataru Real Estate" with no named
tower, so a specific, well-documented, completed Kalpataru tower was chosen as the most findable
match: **Kalpataru Avana**, a single-tower luxury residential project in Parel, Mumbai. This cannot
be confirmed as the actual Aware Acoustics engagement without client-side clarification.

## Location

Parel (Parel–Sewri stretch), Mumbai, Maharashtra, India — near Lower Parel/Sewri, behind ITC Grand
Central.

## Summary

Kalpataru Avana is a single 47-floor residential tower on approximately 2.3 acres, offering 3, 4 and
5 BHK apartments and duplexes with 12'10" floor-to-floor heights and east–west orientation for
natural light and cross-ventilation. It was launched in 2017 and is now complete with occupancy
certificate (OC) received. Amenities include a clubhouse, infinity pool, and squash court.
