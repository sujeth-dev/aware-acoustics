# Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport, Lucknow — Research Notes

**RESEARCH ONLY — not for publication.**

## Identification

- Name: Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport (CCSIA), also historically known as Amausi Airport (IATA: LKO)
- Exact location: Amausi, ~14 km south-west of Lucknow city centre, Uttar Pradesh, India
- Operator: Lucknow International Airport Limited (LIAL), a public-private consortium led by Adani Group under a management concession from AAI

## Factual summary

CCSIA's new Integrated Terminal 3 was inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 10 March 2024. The terminal is designed to handle up to 4,000 passengers during peak hours, with a stated capacity of 8 million passengers per annum in its first phase (planned to scale to 13 million ppa in a later phase), and serves both domestic and international flights. The interior design draws on Lucknow's cultural heritage, incorporating Chikankari and Mukaish embroidery motifs on illuminated check-in counters and frosted-glass art depicting scenes from the Ramayana and Mahabharata, alongside named feature areas such as "Swarna Ghats" and "Basant Bahaar" and a skylit atrium.
