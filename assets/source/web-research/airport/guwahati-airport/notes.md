## Identification

- Name: Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport (LGBI), also spelled Lokapriya Gopinath Bardoloi International Airport (IATA: GAU)
- Exact location: Borjhar, ~20 km west of Guwahati city centre, Assam, India
- Operator: Adani Group (via Adani Airport Holdings, consistent with the existing "Adani Group" client field on the duplicate record)

## Factual summary

LGBI is the primary airport of Northeast India, in operation since 1958, and currently has two terminals. A new integrated terminal building (Terminal 2) has recently been developed with an interior design themed around Assam's cultural heritage and landscape — including an internal-forest motif, bamboo artefacts, a "craft village," and a large double-height entrance space referred to as the "Namaskar Atrium." Terminal 2 has been reported as recognised with an International Architecture Award (2025) and named among the "World's Most Beautiful Airports" for 2026. Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated a new terminal building at the airport per December 2025 government press coverage.
