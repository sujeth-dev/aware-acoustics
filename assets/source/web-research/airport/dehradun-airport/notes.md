# Dehradun Airport (Jolly Grant Airport) — Research Notes

**RESEARCH ONLY — not for publication.**

## Identification

- Name: Dehradun Airport, commonly known as Jolly Grant Airport (IATA: DED)
- Exact location: Jolly Grant / Jauligrant, ~25 km south-east of Dehradun city, Uttarakhand, India
- Operator: Airports Authority of India (AAI)

## Factual summary

A new terminal building was developed by AAI in two phases; Phase I (28,729 sqm) opened first, after which the old terminal was demolished to make way for Phase II. The completed New Terminal Building (Phase II) was inaugurated on 14 February 2024 by Uttarakhand CM Pushkar Singh Dhami and Union Minister of Civil Aviation Jyotiraditya Scindia, at an estimated cost of ₹486 crore. The combined terminal now covers approximately 42,776 sqm, with a concourse, check-in area, security hold and arrival lounge on the ground floor, 48 check-in counters, self-check-in kiosks and in-line baggage screening in the departures area. The terminal's column design is inspired by the Brahmakamal, Uttarakhand's state flower.
