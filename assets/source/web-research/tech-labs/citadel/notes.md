# Citadel — Citadel Securities India, WeWork Two Horizon Centre (Gurgaon)

**Company/facility:** Citadel Securities India Markets Private Limited (Citadel Securities' India
technology/trading office). Note: this is Citadel Securities (the market-making firm), the entity
with a confirmed India registration — not the separate Citadel LLC hedge fund, for which no dedicated
India office could be confirmed.

**Location:** Suite 4B112, WeWork Two Horizon Centre, DLF Golf Course Road, DLF Phase 5, Gurgaon
(Gurugram), Haryana 122002, India.

**Summary:** Citadel Securities' India entity is registered at a WeWork-operated coworking suite
inside the "Two Horizon Centre" tower, a large multi-tenant commercial building developed by DLF on
Golf Course Road, Gurgaon. Public reporting (Bloomberg, Nov 2024) describes a small but growing team —
roughly 10 employees at the time, with plans to add traders and an operations head. This is not a
dedicated, purpose-built Citadel facility; Citadel occupies a single suite within a shared building
used by many unrelated companies.
