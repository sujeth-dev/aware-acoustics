# Godrej — Godrej Appliances R&D Centre, Pirangut (Pune)

**Company/facility:** Godrej Appliances (a business of Godrej & Boyce / Godrej Enterprises Group) —
Research & Development Centre, Pirangut.

**Location:** Pirangut, Pune, Maharashtra, India.

**Summary:** Godrej Appliances expanded its Pirangut R&D facility with a new ~43,000 sq ft "R&D
Centre Annexe," inaugurated in June 2024 by Jamshyd N. Godrej (Chairman & Managing Director,
Godrej & Boyce). The site holds NABL-accredited testing labs, including a Food and Microbiology
Lab used to study food behaviour, preservation and hygiene for refrigeration/appliance testing.
Cumulative R&D investment at Pirangut is reported at close to Rs 100 crore.
