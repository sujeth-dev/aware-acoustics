# ABB — ABB Innovation Center (AIC), Bengaluru

**Company/facility:** ABB Innovation Center (AIC) — comprising ABB's Corporate Technology Centre,
India Operations Centre and Process Automation Digital arms.

**Location:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

**Summary:** Inaugurated/expanded in March 2022, the AIC is described by ABB as one of its largest
R&D and engineering facilities globally: roughly 330,000 sq ft across 8 floors, housing about 2,500
technologists (engineers, data scientists, architects, domain and analytics specialists) developing
AI/ML, data-analytics and automation technology for customers in 50+ countries. The building is
conceptualised as a green building targeting LEED Platinum certification.
