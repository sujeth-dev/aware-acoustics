# ETV — ETV Network / ETV Bharat, Ramoji Film City

**Company/facility:** ETV Network (Telugu-pioneer TV channel group) and ETV Bharat (its digital-first
multilingual news platform), both part of the Ramoji Group.

**Location:** Ramoji Film City, Anaspur Village, Hayathnagar Mandal, R.R. District, Hyderabad,
Telangana 501512, India.

**Summary:** ETV's channels and ETV Bharat's newsroom operate from a campus built inside Ramoji Film
City, the Guinness World Records-certified largest film studio complex in the world (over 1,600
acres, 47 soundstages), which is owned by the same parent, Ramoji Group. ETV Bharat's production
pipeline runs on a Grass Valley GV Stratus video production/content-management system for ingest,
asset management and multi-language publishing across 13 languages.
