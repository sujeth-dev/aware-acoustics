# Alexa — Amazon Hyderabad Campus (HYD13)

**Company/facility:** Amazon.com — Amazon Hyderabad Campus, building HYD13 (part of Amazon's broader
Hyderabad site, which includes Amazon Science teams working on Alexa speech recognition, NLP and
AI research alongside AWS, Amazon Store and other Amazon India functions).

**Location:** Nanakramguda, Financial District, Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

**Summary:** Opened 21 August 2019, HYD13 is Amazon's first company-owned campus outside the US and
is reported as the single largest Amazon-owned office building in the world (approx. 1.8 million sq ft
of office space, capacity for roughly 15,000+ employees). Amazon's Hyderabad site hosts "Science at
Amazon" teams whose published research areas include automatic speech recognition and NLP — the
technologies underlying Alexa — alongside many other Amazon businesses. There is no separate
free-standing "Alexa lab" building; Alexa-related science and engineering work is one of several
functions housed within this campus.
