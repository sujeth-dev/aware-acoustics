# Johnson & Johnson — J&J Institute, Chennai

**Company/facility:** Johnson & Johnson Institute (J&J MedTech professional medical education
facility), Chennai.

**Location:** Johnson & Johnson Institute, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600113, India.

**Summary:** The J&J Institute network provides hands-on professional education for healthcare
practitioners. The Chennai location includes multiple dry and wet labs (accommodating 12–18
participants), classrooms, a faculty lounge and conference facilities, used for surgical/medtech
training in partnership with the larger Mumbai J&J Institute location (per public J&J Institute
location pages).
