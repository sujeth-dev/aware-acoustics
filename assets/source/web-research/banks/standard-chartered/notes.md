# Standard Chartered — Standard Chartered Bank India (Kolkata)

**Building identified:** Standard Chartered Bank Building (historic Chartered Bank of India, Australia & China building), at the corner of Netaji Subhas Road (formerly Clive Street) and India Exchange Place, B.B.D. Bagh (Dalhousie Square), Kolkata.
**Exact location:** 19 N.S. Road, B.B.D. Bagh, Kolkata, West Bengal, India (this was the bank's long-standing branch address before Standard Chartered relocated its main Kolkata branch to 4 Little Russell Street in August 2025, its largest branch premises in Eastern India).

**Summary:** The B.B.D. Bagh building was constructed in 1908 to a design by architect Edward Thornton for Sir Rajen Mookerjee's Martin & Co., originally leased to the Chartered Bank of India, Australia and China (formed 1853); it features a Byzantine-influenced red-brick-and-Porbunder-stone facade with twin towers (110 ft and 135 ft). The Chartered Bank merged with Standard Bank of British South Africa in 1969 to form Standard Chartered Bank, which continued operating from this Kolkata building — a tenant of the same building, Bird & Co., is where actor Amitabh Bachchan worked before his film career. Standard Chartered has since relocated its main/largest Kolkata branch to 4 Little Russell Street (opened 26 August 2025), described as blending "old world charm with best-in-class infrastructure," but no reusable photography of that newer branch was found.
