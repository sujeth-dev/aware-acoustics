# Equitas — Equitas Small Finance Bank (India)

**Building identified:** Equitas Towers, the bank's new corporate headquarters.
**Exact location:** Old Door Nos. 190/1, 190/A and 190/B (originally Old Door No. 190), New Door Nos. 8, 10 and 12, Mount Road, Little Mount, Saidapet, Chennai – 600015, Tamil Nadu, India.

**Summary:** Equitas Small Finance Bank Ltd formally inaugurated Equitas Towers on 7 September 2026 as its new corporate/administrative headquarters, replacing its former head-office address at Spencer Plaza, Mount Road, Chennai. Former RBI Governor Dr. C. Rangarajan inaugurated the premises. The facility houses management and central administrative functions rather than retail banking services; the bank's public statements did not disclose capex, headcount, or migration schedule.
