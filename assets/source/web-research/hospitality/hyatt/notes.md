## Location
Hebbal, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India (near Hebbal Lake, Kirloskar Business Park and Manyata Tech Park).

## Summary
Hyatt Centric Hebbal Bengaluru is a 152-room Hyatt-branded hotel in north Bengaluru's business/tech corridor. It has eight customizable event spaces including a ballroom with a stated capacity of 450 guests, featuring a soundproof partition and a naturally lit pre-function area. Positioned for both corporate and social/wedding events.
