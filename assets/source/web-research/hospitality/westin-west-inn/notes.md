## Location
Powai, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India (lakefront, on Powai Lake).

## Summary
The Westin Mumbai Powai Lake is a Marriott-operated Westin-branded 5-star hotel with 604 guest rooms and suites, many with Powai Lake views. Its column-free Grand Ballroom accommodates up to 1,800 guests and is equipped with advanced AV capabilities, making it one of Mumbai's larger hotel event venues. The property is positioned as a wellness-oriented business and leisure hotel.
