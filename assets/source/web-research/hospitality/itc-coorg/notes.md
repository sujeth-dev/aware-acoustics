# ITC Coorg / WelcomHeritage Ayatana, Coorg

## Identification caveat
No currently operating property is marketed as "ITC Coorg, a Luxury Collection Resort." Research strongly suggests the brief refers to the ITC-associated luxury resort in Kodagu (Coorg) district that operated under ITC/WelcomHeritage management and is now marketed independently as **Ayatana Coorg** (formerly "ITC WelcomHeritage Ayatana, Coorg"). Multiple travel listings (TiCATi, Veekends) still index it under the name "ITC WelcomHeritage Ayatana, Coorg." This identification is provisional — confirm against actual client/contract records before treating it as the correct property.

## Location
Aratt Estate, Kumarahalli village, Somwarpet taluk, Kodagu (Coorg) district, Karnataka, India — approx. 100 acres of Western Ghats rainforest, ~57 km from Madikeri.

## Summary
A luxury eco-resort with 36 standard cottages, 13 luxury suites and a presidential suite (roughly 50 rooms total), built using a prefabricated, low-impact construction method to minimize disturbance to the rainforest, waterfall and existing trees on site. Notable features include a private waterfall and infinity pool overlooking it, plus an on-site zip-lining course. Near Kumara Parvatha, Mallalli Falls and Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary.
