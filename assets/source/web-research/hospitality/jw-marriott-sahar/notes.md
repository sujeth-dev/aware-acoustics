## Location
Andheri East, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India (near Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport).

## Summary
JW Marriott Mumbai Sahar is a JW Marriott-branded 5-star hotel with 588 rooms and suites. It has 11 event spaces including a pillar-less Grand Ballroom (~10,350 sq ft, up to 1,000 guests for corporate events) and Junior Ballrooms (~3,900 sq ft), plus the JW Lawns outdoor venue (capacity up to 1,500). It is a well-known wedding, conference, and banqueting venue in Mumbai's airport district.
