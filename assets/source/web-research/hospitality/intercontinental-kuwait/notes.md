## Identification caveat
Kuwait has multiple InterContinental-branded projects: (1) the established **InterContinental Kuwait** on Gulf Street, Salmiya (Area No. 71, Block No. 2A) — the property researched here; (2) **InterContinental Kuwait Downtown**, a 2012-announced, apparently never-completed project; (3) **InterContinental Kuwait Al Thuraya City**, a newer/separate 112-room property. Confirm which property matches the actual client engagement before treating this research as authoritative.

## Location
Gulf Street, Area No. 71, Block No. 2A, Salmiya, Kuwait City, Kuwait (Gulf-front, ~10 min from downtown, ~20 min from Kuwait International Airport).

## Summary
InterContinental Kuwait is a long-established 5-star hotel on the Gulf Road in Salmiya, with rooms and suites overlooking the Arabian Gulf. It includes conference and banquet/meeting facilities (boardroom and meeting-room space observed in source imagery) and an all-day dining restaurant ("Stair 27") on the mezzanine level overlooking the lobby.
