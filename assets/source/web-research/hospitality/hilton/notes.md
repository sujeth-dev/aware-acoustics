Not a duplicate — no existing Hilton record in `data/projects.json`. Selected as a well-documented Hilton property in India (Guindy, Chennai) for this brief; treat identification as provisional until confirmed against actual client records.

## Location
124/1 Jawaharlal Nehru Road, Guindy, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600032, India.

## Summary
Hilton Chennai is a 5-star, 10-storey hotel that opened 28 February 2011, with 204 rooms (including Executive Suites, Junior Suites, Hilton Deluxe and Hilton Executive/King rooms). Its Hilton Grand Ballroom is a divisible ~445 sq m space with a 5.9 m ceiling, holding up to 750 guests, plus additional meeting rooms and a boardroom.
