## Web research trail
- **The Great Eastern Shipping Co. Ltd** ("GE Shipping") is confirmed real: India's largest
  private-sector shipping company, founded 1948, headquartered at Ocean House, 134/A B Wing, BDD Chawls,
  Worli, Mumbai 400018 (Wikipedia; company registry/business-directory listings).
- No museum branded under the Great Eastern Shipping name could be found on the company's own site
  (greatship.com), in press coverage, in its CSR Foundation materials, or on Wikipedia/Wikidata.
- Candidates investigated and ruled out as **not** a confirmed match:
  - **Great Eastern Institute of Maritime Studies (GEIMS), Lonavala** — a real maritime *training
    institute* founded by GE Shipping in 2006 (18-acre campus, Tungarli Village, Lonavala 410403). This
    is a training facility, not a museum, so it does not match the "museum" categorisation used
    internally, even though it is the company's most prominent public-facing physical facility.
  - **Maritime Mumbai Museum Society** (mmms.in) — a separate, multi-stakeholder civic initiative
    proposing a Mumbai maritime museum at Oyster Rock; no confirmed Great Eastern Shipping sponsorship
    link found.
  - **T.S. Rahaman Marine Museum**, Nhava Sheva — an unrelated training-ship heritage museum (not tied
    to GE Shipping in any source found).
  - Searches for a GE Shipping-owned museum ship, in-house corporate heritage gallery at Ocean House, or
    a CSR-funded museum all returned no results.
