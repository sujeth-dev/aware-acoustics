# Siemens (Siemens Healthineers) — Corporate Office (Research Only)

**Company:** Siemens Healthineers — medical technology arm of Siemens (imaging, diagnostics, digital health); one of Siemens's largest single-site investments globally.
**Likely building/campus:** Siemens Healthineers Innovation Hub / Global Campus, Bengaluru — designed by Eller + Eller Architekten.
**City/Country:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

## Summary
Announced in 2020 with a reported investment of roughly Rs 1,300 crore, the Bengaluru campus combines Siemens Healthineers' existing R&D centre with a large innovation hub and imaging-equipment manufacturing space — described as the company's largest location in the world by area once fully built out (around 114,000 sqm across phases). The design features terraced buildings of varying heights linked by skywalks and sky gardens, extensive natural ventilation/daylighting, and photovoltaic-integrated atrium canopies.
