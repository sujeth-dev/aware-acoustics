# ABB — Corporate Office (Research Only)

**Company:** ABB Ltd — Swiss-Swedish multinational, electrification and automation technology.
**Likely building/campus:** ABB India Nelamangala campus "Main Office Block," Bengaluru (an IGBC-certified green campus). ABB India also relocated its corporate/business office in 2021 to a new integrated building named "Disha" within its Peenya campus, Bengaluru — see note below.
**City/Country:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

## Summary
ABB India operates several Bengaluru-area sites: a Peenya campus (registered office and, since 2021, the "Disha" building housing the corporate office plus Electrification, Motion and Process Automation business offices — LEED Gold-targeted, ~5,000 ABB products used as a live building-automation demonstration) and a Nelamangala manufacturing campus that has received the IGBC's highest ("Platinum") Green Factory Building rating. The exterior image saved here shows the Nelamangala campus's Main Office Block, the clearest available reference photo; a labeled photo of the newer "Disha" corporate office exterior was also found but only at very low resolution (400x230px) — see alternate source link below.
