# Larsen & Toubro (L&T) — Corporate Office (Research Only)

**Company:** Larsen & Toubro Limited — Indian multinational conglomerate: engineering, construction (EPC), manufacturing, technology and financial services.
**Likely building/campus:** A. M. Naik Tower, L&T's Powai campus (Saki Vihar Road / JVLR junction), Mumbai. (Note: the brief suggested Mahape, but the identifiable, documented corporate-office tower is A. M. Naik Tower at Powai — L&T's oldest and most prominent Mumbai campus; Mahape/Navi Mumbai hosts L&T Infotech/development-center facilities rather than a distinct named HQ tower.)
**City/Country:** Mumbai (Powai), Maharashtra, India.

## Summary
A. M. Naik Tower is a 15-storey, ~1.03 million sq ft "smart" green office building inaugurated in December 2020 at L&T's Powai campus, houses roughly 4,500 group employees, and holds Green building (Platinum-track) credentials with an IoT-based building management system, digital ceilings and occupancy-based HVAC/lighting control. The tower also houses "Planet L&T," an 11,500 sq ft digitally-enabled corporate experience centre used to showcase the company's history and capabilities to visitors.
