# Google — Corporate Office (Research Only)

**Company:** Google (Alphabet Inc.) — search, advertising, cloud computing, consumer hardware and software.
**Likely building/campus:** Ananta, Google India's Bengaluru campus (Mahadevapura, Bengaluru).
**City/Country:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

## Summary
Ananta is Google India's purpose-built campus in Mahadevapura, Bengaluru, opened to accommodate over 7,000 employees. It features a sculpted, asymmetric facade intended to maximize natural light and views, and includes one of India's largest installations of electro-chromic (SageGlass) smart glass. The campus was developed in collaboration with Indian architects/designers (facade and interiors partly by Broadway Malyan) as a showcase for sustainable, large-floorplate workplace design.
