# Microsoft — Corporate Office (Research Only)

**Company:** Microsoft Corporation — software, cloud computing (Azure), productivity tools, devices.
**Likely building/campus:** Microsoft India Development Center (MSIDC), Bangalore Campus — floors of Luxor North Tower, Bagmane Capital Campus, Mahadevapura, Bengaluru East.
**City/Country:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

## Summary
Microsoft's Bangalore Campus is one of its India Development Center engineering hubs, occupying the Luxor North Tower within the Bagmane Capital Campus in the KR Puram/Mahadevapura area. Microsoft's own materials describe the interior design as blending ethnic Indian architectural motifs with modern structural materials in an open-plan office layout, with accessibility features for differently-abled staff built into the design.
