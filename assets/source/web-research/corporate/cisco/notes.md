# Cisco — Corporate Office (Research Only)

**Company:** Cisco Systems Inc. — networking hardware, software, and cybersecurity.
**Likely building/campus:** Cisco Bengaluru campus (Global Capability Centre / "GCC"), a ~25-acre, nine-building campus off Outer Ring Road, Bengaluru — one of Cisco's largest sites outside the US.
**City/Country:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

## Summary
Cisco's Bengaluru campus is described by Cisco as home to over 10,000 "Cisconians" across nine interconnected buildings (linked by sky bridges) spanning almost 5 million sq ft — it is Cisco's largest campus outside its US headquarters. The images sourced here are from a design agency's case study of environmental/campus graphics work done across Cisco's Bengaluru buildings (project "VTV").
